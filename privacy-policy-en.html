Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 25, 2026

Tsumugiworks ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit [tsumugiworks.com] (the "Site"), how we use it, and what choices you have.

Please note: all purchases of Tsumugiworks products are completed on Etsy, not on this Site. This Site does not process payments, collect shipping addresses, or handle orders directly.

1. Who We Are

Tsumugiworks designs cross stitch patterns and indigo-dyed (aizome) textile goods, based in Hiroshima, Japan. If you have any questions about this policy or your personal information, contact us at tsumugiworks25@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you give us directly: your name and email address, if you sign up for our newsletter or contact us through a form on this Site.
  • Information collected automatically: anonymous visit data such as pages viewed and general location, collected through Cloudflare Web Analytics (see Section 4). This does not include any information that identifies you personally.

We do not collect payment information, shipping addresses, or order details on this Site — those are handled entirely by Etsy when you make a purchase there.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • respond to your questions and messages;
  • send you our newsletter, if you've opted in — you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email;
  • understand, in aggregate, how visitors use the Site so we can improve it;
  • detect and prevent misuse of the Site.

4. Cookies and Analytics

This Site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how visitors use the Site. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies and does not track individual visitors across websites — it only collects anonymous, aggregate statistics (such as page views and general location by country). No personally identifying information is collected through this tool.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We share information with the following service providers, only as needed for them to do their job:

  • Etsy: all purchases of our products happen on Etsy. Any payment, shipping, and order information you provide there is handled directly by Etsy under Etsy's own Privacy Policy, not this one.
  • Systeme.io: our email newsletter is managed through Systeme.io, if you subscribe.
  • Cloudflare: hosts this Site and provides Cloudflare Web Analytics (see Section 4).

These providers only receive the information they need to perform their service and are not permitted to use it for their own marketing purposes. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Data Retention

Newsletter contact information (name and email) is kept until you unsubscribe or request deletion. We do not retain payment or order information, since those are handled by Etsy.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and request a copy;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate information;
  • ask us to delete your personal information;
  • opt out of marketing emails at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at tsumugiworks25@gmail.com. If you are in the EU/UK or California, you have additional rights under GDPR or the CCPA respectively; we will respond to verified requests within the time required by applicable law.

8. International Visitors

Tsumugiworks is based in Japan, and information you provide may be processed and stored in Japan or in the countries where our service providers operate. By using the Site, you understand that your information may be transferred outside your home country.

9. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including relying on trusted, secure third-party providers for payment and hosting. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. If changes are significant, we will provide additional notice where appropriate.
