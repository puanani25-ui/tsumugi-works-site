Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: August 25, 2026

This page explains what information is collected by Tsumugiworks' Threads integration app (used to automate posting) and how to have it deleted.

1. What This App Collects

This app was built to automate posting on Tsumugiworks' own Threads account. It stores only the access token needed to connect to Threads and the minimal account information required to publish posts. It does not collect information from any third-party accounts.

2. How to Delete Your Data

You can remove any information this app has stored in either of the following ways:

  • Revoke access from Threads directly: In the Threads app, go to Settings → Account → Apps and Websites, and remove this app's access. This immediately invalidates any access token we have stored.
  • Request deletion by email: Contact us at tsumugiworks25@gmail.com and we will locate and delete any stored information.

3. Timeline

Revoking access takes effect immediately. Email requests are completed within 30 days of confirmation.
